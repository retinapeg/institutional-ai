# Institutional AI

What if a team of AI agents worked like an institution rather than a group chat, with independent reports, peer challenge, dissent kept on the record and a Director who makes the final call?

**Status:** Frozen prototype, superseded by [institutional-workbench](https://github.com/retinapeg/institutional-workbench). Every agent reply is scripted, and no model is called.

- Runs a toy fleet-optimisation mission end to end: sealed initial reports, mandatory peer reviews, revisions and a hash-linked audit log.
- It left an open question: do expert-role prompts help? I later tested that in institutional-workbench, and no role-specific benefit was established.
- Because the specialists are scripted, it says nothing about how real models behave in this structure.
